## Dark social expansion

Implemented in the local web demo: an editorial dark home, official imagery and metadata for all 19 projects, completed/ongoing filters, source links, synthetic Skylark 101/102/103 register, consent-based directory, profile-photo/occupation/bio/contact editing, profile locks, friend request/accept/decline/remove/cancel, blocking, project/friend/network post audiences, photo posts, likes/comments, reports and scoped moderation. Locks are enforced on server responses and post access, including after unfriend/move-out. Contact visibility is separate from friendship and never defaults to public.

Insta Services: eight service categories; resident requests for verified MDP flat memberships, including an owner’s ongoing project; MDP service desk; trade-matched assignment; exclusive active technician job; assigned-only technician queue; requested/assigned/en-route/in-progress/completed/cancelled timeline. No real technicians are dispatched. Six demo specialists are seeded; categories without a specialist remain pending until one is provisioned.

Native source now has the same dark direction, project/flat exploration, resident profiles, photo picker, profile/privacy editing, friend requests/acceptance, daily photo posts and likes, and service booking/tracking. Native comments, reports/block controls and dispatch staff UI remain web-only. Latest native bundles are verification artifacts, not signed builds.

Supabase social/service migration adds default-private tables, redacted-profile RPC, friendship/block/post visibility helpers, service constraints and private social image storage. These migrations have not been executed against a live Postgres instance. Production RPC coverage and adapters still need implementation.

## Project governance & MDP Exchange — implemented extension

Local SQLite runtime and web UI now include:

- **Numeric flat migration:** stable IDs and relationships; a complete proposed mapping is checked before any label updates. Ambiguous/missing suffixes or same-project collisions abort conversion with a correction mapping. Catalog counts never generate flats. Global demo banner remains.
- **Exchange:** persisted sale/free/wanted/swap listings, five validated JPEG/PNG photos, project-default / explicit network audience, preview/draft/publish/edit/reserved/sold/withdrawn/derived expiry, search/category/type/condition/project/price filters, saves, bounded pages, persisted private enquiries with six-second refresh, reports, blocking and platform moderation. No stock, bike, checkout, escrow or payment integration. Seller response contains name/id only; optional social-profile access still follows the separate directory/profile permissions.
- **Committee:** project-specific President/VP invitations, resident acceptance, administrator activation, terms, expiry, revoke/resign/replace, separate people and independent review. Only local `platform` appointment authority can appoint; it gains no financial access. Nest has clearly synthetic Kiran/Ananya examples. Other projects start vacant.
- **My Project:** project selector for active approved memberships and verified ownership records, own dues, monthly statements, common bills, private/public questions, notices, meetings/minutes/decisions, non-election opinion polls, configured assets and upcoming/overdue renewal reminders, recurring bill templates, category budgets, pending-work handover and audit export. A member with appointments receives scoped committee controls without losing resident identity. New committee questions and MDP specialist requests use separate routes. Previous legacy records remain read-only in My Home history.
- **Billing:** explicit project activation/start date/policy, fixed or verified-area charges, configured special assessment/exemption/opening dues, immutable payer per invoice, draft/review/publish/reconcile/close, unique monthly flat bills, published notices, versioned signed charge/credit notes, direct-to-project payment submissions, independent manual verification, partial payments, receipts and advances applied without new cash. Screenshots are never bank verification. No automatic penalty or expense-to-flat charge allocation is performed.
- **Accounting:** accrued vendor bills and actual vendor payments are separate. Paid/unpaid/partial balances, duplicate bill/reference checks, independent review thresholds, diesel litres/rate checks, common-service link uniqueness, encrypted private bill/proof attachments, separately uploaded redacted resident copies, monthly CSV/print and annual CSV. Approved signed vendor corrections post to an open month and preserve old records/snapshots. Original bills remain immutable. Reserves have separate opening balances and explicit same-project transfers; transfers are separate from vendor expenditure. Other receipts require a project-record confirmation. Refunds and bank feeds are not implemented.
- **Privacy:** project membership or verified ownership plus current appointment checked on every privileged call; platform/association/global staff do not bypass it. Residents see their own invoice/payment records and published aggregate accounts/redacted bills. Other residents’ proofs, raw payee details, personal vaults, personal loans and locked social profiles remain separate. Liability changes affect future invoices; old tenant records never move to a replacement tenant.

### Production foundation and configuration

Migration `202610180003_project_governance_exchange.sql` adds scoped records, composite foreign keys, committee and participant helpers, default-deny financial writes, resident-only financial projections, private buckets and RLS. No staff-role shortcut reads project finances. No real appointments, flats, policies, approvals or balances are seeded. This migration has **not** been run against live PostgreSQL; the current runtime is still local SQLite. Production server adapters/RPCs, transactional audit hardening, scanner-backed storage/signed downloads, identity/invitation verification, retention, notification workers and staging RLS integration tests are required before launch. Do not expose the local demo publicly.

Payment instructions are intentionally unconfigured until an authorized committee supplies its own reviewed destination. MDP collects no maintenance money. No statutory accounting/tax compliance, real dispatch, live bank reconciliation or first-in-Hyderabad claim is made.

Resident home now surfaces own outstanding bills, project notices, relevant Exchange listings and active MDP specialist requests ahead of the wider community journal. Revoked committee uploaders lose access to linked project-bill originals; their own personal proofs and published redacted copies remain separately authorized.

## MDP Daily — grocery-first monetization pilot

Resident Home now leads with the MDP Daily storefront rather than the community journal. It has an original grocery-basket illustration, six departments, cross-category breakfast/monthly-cupboard shopping shortcuts, search, stocked/proposed range selection, product art and the existing working cart. Community and private tools remain accessible through navigation and home shortcuts. Native Home also leads with category/search and add/remove controls, with the existing Shop checkout and order history retained.

A balanced **76-SKU proposed assortment** has explicit unit prices and monthly volumes totaling **₹3,00,000 in merchandise sales**: ₹60k dairy/eggs, ₹75k staples, ₹45k oils/masalas, ₹45k produce, ₹36k breakfast/snacks/drinks and ₹39k home/personal care. These are planning inputs, not confirmed supplier quotes, sales forecasts or verified household spending. The 130-family / ₹6.5 lakh market assumption comes from the user. No real brands, procurement or cold-chain readiness are invented.

The warehouse/platform-only **Daily retail plan** shows the full proposed product list, category allocations, monthly quantities, opening-cover targets, CSV export, actual delivered grocery sales (excluding fees) and editable illustrative margin/operating-cost arithmetic. Revenue, gross margin, listed-cost remainder and break-even are distinguished. Changes to a scenario do not change catalog prices or the fixed assortment plan. Apartment committee and resident users cannot fetch this business plan.

Original SKU IDs, prices, batches, stock and orders are preserved. **62 new SKUs start at zero stock**, with no fabricated batch arrivals. Activating a proposed item requires privileged receiving with confirmed buying/selling prices, supplier, quantity and future expiry. Resident stock availability excludes expired batches; existing FEFO, checkout idempotency, coverage and rider limits remain. The SQL assortment/planning migration is an unexecuted production foundation with no real products seeded or client write grants.

Detailed proposed range: `outputs/grocery/MDP-Daily-300000-product-plan.csv` and `outputs/grocery/MDP-Daily-plan.md`. Prices, pack choices, buying costs and local demand still need supplier/resident validation. Pack art is illustrative. No subscription, ten-minute delivery promise, stock purchasing, payment gateway or real dispatch was added.
