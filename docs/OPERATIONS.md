# One warehouse, two electric bikes

## Before opening a pilot

Choose one occupied, authorized MDP project from a verified resident register. Confirm flat addresses, warehouse operating hours, reachable delivery zone, actual rider shifts, product sourcing and expiry practices. Supply real prices and costs. Set service flags explicitly; completion status is not delivery authorization.

Start with packaged/fixed-weight essentials. The demo does not support variable-weight settlement. No instant-delivery promise. Slot capacity starts at three orders per available rider and must be adjusted from observed pilot data, battery capacity and travel times.

## Daily workflow

1. Receive stock against a supplier batch and real expiry; inspect quality and pack sizes.
2. Check near-expiry stock before opening service. Use first-expiring-first-out picking. Never extend expiry during restocking.
3. Confirm both staff shifts, EV-01/EV-02 range, charge plans, and safe carrying capacity. A charging/off-shift bike contributes no slot capacity.
4. Review placed orders, pick and pack. If an item is unavailable, contact resident; do not make an unconsented substitution. Current demo does not execute substitutions.
5. Assign packed orders to an eligible rider. Up to three active deliveries per rider in the demo. Dispatch remains separate from carpooling.
6. Confirm delivery with resident code. Demo `123456` is public and must be replaced before any real service. Reconcile actual COD cash independently.
7. Failed delivery returns to warehouse inspection. Returned items are not automatically restocked. Check temperature, seal, safety and expiry before disposition.
8. Reconcile orders, cash, returned stock, waste and complaints at closing; schedule charging and purchasing.

## Margin interpretation

Demo contribution = delivered item sales + delivery charges − unit costs − estimated picking − estimated delivery. Estimates are ₹10 picking and ₹25 delivery per completed order. COD fees are zero in the demo. This is **not net profit**: warehouse rent, salaries, taxes, utilities, loss, actual refund fees and financing are not modeled.

## Resident support

Keep tickets scoped to the correct association. Verify move-outs and revoke residence access promptly. Do not ask for residents' vault files as a shortcut to resolving a delivery or association issue. Avoid putting personal details into notifications or audit text.

## Expanding

Measure baskets per day, on-time percentage, stock-outs, returns, spoilage, repeat use and actual cost per delivery before adding projects. Configure the zone and slots before promising coverage. Add bikes/staff/warehouse capacity only from validated demand and economics.

Post reasoned vendor credits/supplementary corrections as linked records in an open period. Close a reconciled month to preserve its statement snapshot. Do not backdate movements into a closed month. Monthly cash balances and accrued billing/expenditure have distinct labels; reserve opening balances and explicit same-project transfers are separate. ## Daily retail plan

Choose **Warehouse team → Daily retail plan** for the 76-SKU ₹3 lakh monthly demand plan, category mix, CSV export and illustrative economics. Use **Ananya → Your daily store** for the customer storefront. Association/committee appointment powers do not grant retail-plan access.

The demand plan is not a stock receipt or purchase order. Start with short replenishment cycles: proposed dairy/produce/bread/batter cover is one day, other categories typically 7–10 days. Actual expiry/storage is supplier-specific. Opening quantities must be adjusted for physical stock, actual sales, losses, supplier lead times and verified cold storage. Do not procure the full monthly target at once.

In Stock & receiving, new proposed items need both **unit buying cost** and **confirmed selling price**, plus supplier, quantity and expiry. The transaction records the batch and activates only that item’s demo catalog state. Confirm pack/brand details before real use. Existing products do not have their prices or stock overwritten by assortment migration. Receiving and operator actuals are still local-demo records; production procurement and financial integrations are unconfigured.
